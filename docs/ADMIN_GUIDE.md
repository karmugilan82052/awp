# Administrator Guide — Smart Agri Waste Marketplace

This guide covers governance, listing moderation, user KYC verification, escrow settlement, dispute arbitration, and audit reporting.

---

## 1. Accessing the Administrator Console

- Switch your active role to **Admin** via the top navbar dropdown or log in with admin credentials.
- Navigate to the **Admin Console** (`#admin-dashboard`).

---

## 2. Listing Moderation & Approval

- Click on the **"Listing Moderation"** tab.
- Review new agricultural waste submissions:
  - Check photo clarity and stated crop species.
  - Verify stated moisture level matches physical storage conditions.
  - Ensure price falls within realistic regional market benchmarks.
- Click **"Approve"** to publish the listing live across national search, or **"Reject"** with specific feedback for the farmer.

---

## 3. User KYC & Verification

- Click on the **"User KYC Verification"** tab.
- Inspect farmer land holding certificates, FPO registration numbers, and industrial buyer GSTIN IDs.
- Toggle verified badges or suspend fraudulent accounts.

---

## 4. Dispute Arbitration Desk

- Click on the **"Dispute Arbitration"** tab.
- Review buyer/seller dispute tickets (e.g. moisture discrepancy, weighbridge shortage, or transit delays).
- Inspect uploaded lab test certificates and weighbridge gatepasses.
- Issue binding arbitration findings and mark tickets as **Resolved**.

---

## 5. Category & Conversion Rules Manager

- Click on the **"Category Manager"** tab.
- Add new agricultural waste categories (e.g., *Areca Leaf Sheaths*, *Coffee Husk*).
- Define suitable transformation applications, storage guidelines, and transport packaging standards.

---

## 6. Report Export & Platform Analytics

- Click on the **"Reports & Exports"** tab.
- Download standardized CSV audit spreadsheets for:
  - Registered Farmers Directory
  - Procurement & Escrow Ledger
  - Sustainability & Carbon Credits Generated
