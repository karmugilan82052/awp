This guide explains how to use the platform as a **Farmer / Producer** or as an **Industrial Buyer / Recycler**.

## 2. Farmer Workflow: Selling Agricultural Waste

### Step 1: Access Listing Form
Click **"+ Sell Agri Waste"** in the top navigation bar.

### Step 2: AI Auto-Classification
- Upload your crop residue photos.
- Click **"✨ Auto-Classify Waste with AI"**.
- The AI deep learning service will automatically identify the waste category (e.g. *Paddy Straw* or *Sugarcane Bagasse*), suggest estimated moisture levels, and provide market price benchmarks (e.g. *₹4,200 - ₹4,800 / Ton*).

### Step 3: Enter Tons, Price & Farm Gate Location
- Specify available quantity in Tons.
- Set minimum order quantity (MOQ) and your expected price.
- Provide farm pickup district, state, and pincode.
- Click **"Publish Agricultural Waste Listing →"**.

### Step 4: Manage Incoming Orders & Receive Payouts
- Visit your **Farmer Hub** (`#farmer-dashboard`).
- View incoming buyer orders.
- Accept orders and track transporter truck arrival at your farm gate.
- Receive direct bank escrow settlements upon weighbridge verification.

---

## 3. Buyer Workflow: Sourcing Biomass & Placing Orders

### Step 1: Browse the Marketplace
- Navigate to **Marketplace** (`#marketplace`).
- Use the left sidebar to filter by:
  - Waste Category (Paddy straw, bagasse, coconut husk, cow dung, etc.)
  - State & District
  - Price per ton ceiling slider
  - Minimum available quantity
  - Quality Grade (Grade A, Export quality)
- Toggle between **Grid View**, **List View**, and **GIS Map View** with live farm location pins!

### Step 2: Evaluate Technical Specifications
- Click on any waste listing to view:
  - High-resolution photo gallery
  - Moisture level, packaging type, storage facility
  - Verified farmer credentials & star ratings
  - **Smart Recommended Uses** (Bio-CNG, pellets, paper, animal feed)
  - Distance in km from your plant

### Step 3: Add to Procurement Cart & Escrow Checkout
- Choose your required tons.
- Click **"Add to Procurement Cart"** or **"Buy Now"**.
- Review dynamic logistics freight calculations.
- Select your preferred payment method (**UPI QR Code**, **Credit/Debit Card**, **Net Banking**, or **AgroWallet**).
- Click **"Authorize Escrow Payment"** to confirm your order with celebratory confetti!

### Step 4: Real-Time Shipment Tracking & Review
- Track live order status on the 9-stage fulfillment timeline.
- Download GST tax invoices with 1 click.
- After delivery completion, submit a **5-Star Multi-Criteria Review** for the seller.
